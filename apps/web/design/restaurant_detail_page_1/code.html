About Pho Thin

Pho Thin by the Lake is a legendary spot in Hanoi, famous for its unique stir-fried beef pho (Phở Bò Tái Lăn). Unlike traditional pho, the beef is quickly sautéed with garlic before being added to the bowl, giving the broth an extra layer of flavor and aroma. This cozy, bustling eatery offers an authentic taste of Hanoian street food culture, beloved by locals and tourists alike.
